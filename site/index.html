Calculation assumptions

Ideal, well-mixed inlet/outlet gas streams; dry mole fractions; comparable reference conditions; inert fraction (all non-O₂, non-CO₂ gases) conserved. The outlet flow is corrected by the inert balance, not assumed equal to the inlet flow.

OUR = Fᵢ yO₂,ᵢ − Fₒ yO₂,ₒ; CER = Fₒ yCO₂,ₒ − Fᵢ yCO₂,ᵢ; RQ = CER/OUR when OUR is positive.

This is a process-wide gas balance. It does not analyze raw mass spectra, correct for instrument response, or normalize rates by reactor volume. Sensor calibration, humidity removal, leakage, and mixing time affect accuracy.
